Tutorials

Learn how to create and print your own color-by-number art.

01 · Choose a good photo

Use a sharp image with a clear subject and good contrast. Avoid very dark or blurry photos.

02 · Pick your puzzle style

Start with Square or Hexagon. Increase grid size for more detail and choose a smaller color palette for simplicity.

03 · Print your puzzle

Download the PNG or use Print / Save PDF, then choose “Save as PDF” in your browser.

From the Blog

Ideas and tips for your creative projects.

What is a Mystery Mosaic?

A puzzle where numbered geometric cells reveal an image as you color them.

How many colors should I use?

Six to ten colors is a friendly starting point. More colors can preserve extra detail.

Make print-ready puzzle pages

Choose a clear layout and test print at normal size before sharing your design.
